Data Choices & Deletion

Last updated: September 18, 2026

PaceShift has no separate user account. App information may exist locally on your Apple devices, in your private iCloud database, in Apple Health, in Apple’s purchase records, or in support correspondence you chose to send.

Before deleting: export anything you want to keep. Deletion may be permanent. Offload App is not the same as Delete App and normally keeps local documents and data.

1. Delete local PaceShift data

Use the deletion controls shown in your installed version to remove individual sessions or other records. To remove the app’s local container from a device:

  1. Open iPhone or iPad Settings.
  2. Open General → iPhone Storage or iPad Storage.
  3. Select PaceShift.
  4. Choose Delete App, not Offload App.
  5. Confirm deletion.

Deleting the iPhone or iPad app does not automatically erase records held separately by iCloud, Apple Health, or the App Store. Remove the Watch app and widgets separately when needed.

2. Delete PaceShift continuity data from iCloud

Private CloudKit continuity information can remain after the app is deleted. Apple may change the exact labels or arrangement of these controls.

  1. Open Apple device Settings.
  2. Tap your Apple Account name, then iCloud.
  3. Open Storage or Manage Account Storage.
  4. Select PaceShift when it is listed.
  5. Choose the available option to delete PaceShift data from iCloud and confirm.

PaceShift uses your private CloudKit database and has no separate account directory. Support generally cannot identify, access, or erase that private iCloud record remotely for you. Apple provides additional guidance on managing third-party app data in iCloud.

3. Apple Health data and permissions

Deleting PaceShift does not automatically delete workouts or other records already stored in Apple Health.

  1. Open the Health app.
  2. Open your profile or account controls.
  3. Open Apps and Services, Data Access & Devices, or the corresponding privacy screen.
  4. Select PaceShift to review or revoke permissions.
  5. Use Health’s source and record-management controls to delete records you no longer want.

Apple maintains the authoritative Health database and may change the exact menu wording. See Apple’s guidance for managing Health data.

4. Guarded Protection and Screen Time

This section applies only when Guarded Protection appears in your installed version. End any active Guarded session, clear your selection in PaceShift, and revoke PaceShift’s Screen Time authorization in Apple device settings when you no longer want the feature to operate.

When a shield remains unexpectedly active, reopen PaceShift and end the Guarded session. When necessary, revoke the app’s Screen Time authorization before deleting the app and contact support.

5. Other optional permissions

You can withdraw Calendar, Bluetooth, notifications, Live Activities, and other optional permissions in Apple device settings. Withdrawing a permission prevents or limits the related feature but does not delete records already stored elsewhere.

6. Subscriptions and App Store transaction records

Deleting PaceShift does not cancel PaceShift Pro. Manage or cancel through Apple Account subscriptions. Apple retains transaction information under its own legal, fraud-prevention, and accounting obligations. PaceShift cannot delete Apple’s transaction history.

7. Support correspondence

To request deletion or correction of support correspondence controlled by Gavin Naicker, email paceshift.app@gmail.com from the address used for the original message. Include enough detail to locate the correspondence. Reasonable identity verification may be required, and some information may be retained where permitted or required by law.

8. Privacy request

Because PaceShift has no separate account, support cannot retrieve local device information or private iCloud data simply from your name or email address. For information the Developer controls, requests are handled within the period required by applicable law.
