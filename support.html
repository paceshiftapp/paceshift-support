Before contacting support

  1. Open PaceShift Settings and note the version and build number.
  2. Confirm the device is using a supported operating-system version and install available PaceShift updates.
  3. Restart PaceShift and, when appropriate, restart the device.
  4. Write down what you expected, what happened, and the exact steps that reproduce the issue.

Timer, audio, widgets, and Live Activities

  • Confirm the selected cycle contains the expected work and recovery intervals.
  • Review PaceShift cue and audio settings, device volume, Silent mode, Focus mode, and the active audio route.
  • For a missing Live Activity, confirm Live Activities are allowed for PaceShift in Apple device settings, then start a new timer.
  • For a stale widget, open PaceShift, complete a normal action, and allow iOS time to refresh the widget.
  • After an interruption or process termination, reopen PaceShift so it can reconcile saved timer state.

Apple Watch

  1. Confirm the Watch is paired with the iPhone and PaceShift is installed.
  2. Keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi available on both devices.
  3. Open PaceShift on iPhone once, then open PaceShift on Apple Watch.
  4. When state remains stale, safely end the current session, restart both apps, and begin a new session.

Apple Health and compatible heart-rate monitors

PaceShift’s core timer works without Apple Health permission. For Health features, review PaceShift under Apple Health’s Apps and Services or privacy controls and grant only the permissions needed for the feature you choose.

PaceShift supports compatible Bluetooth Low Energy heart-rate monitors that expose the standard Heart Rate service. It does not claim support for cycling power, cadence, speed, smart trainers, foot pods, or ANT+ sensors.

  1. Disconnect the monitor from another app that may be holding its connection.
  2. Wake and wear the monitor according to its manufacturer’s instructions.
  3. Allow PaceShift Bluetooth access when prompted.
  4. Open PaceShift’s heart-rate sensor screen and choose the monitor.
  5. When it does not appear, power-cycle the monitor and restart Bluetooth on the iPhone.

Heart-rate and workout information can be delayed or inaccurate. PaceShift is not a medical device. Stop activity and seek appropriate help for urgent symptoms.

Guarded Protection

This section applies only when Guarded Protection appears in your installed version. When available, Guarded Protection is free and does not require PaceShift Pro.

  1. Open PaceShift Settings and select Guarded Protection.
  2. Request Apple Screen Time access.
  3. Choose the apps, categories, or websites you want to guard.
  4. Start a Guarded session.
  5. End or cancel the session to clear shielding.

When shielding does not clear, reopen PaceShift and end the Guarded session. You can also revoke PaceShift’s Screen Time authorization in Apple device settings. Do not shield an app you may urgently need for safety, medical care, travel, authentication, work, school, communication, or accessibility.

iCloud continuity

  1. Confirm both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account.
  2. Open Apple device Settings → your name → iCloud and confirm PaceShift is allowed to use iCloud.
  3. Connect both devices to the internet.
  4. Open PaceShift on the first device, make one small change, then open PaceShift on the second device.
  5. Allow normal CloudKit propagation time and avoid repeatedly importing the same file.

PaceShift’s continuity filter is designed to keep Health and fitness-bearing records out of its own CloudKit snapshot, so some HIIT or heart-rate history may remain device-specific.

Restore a purchase

  1. Confirm the device is signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  2. Open PaceShift Settings.
  3. Choose Restore Purchases.
  4. Allow StoreKit time to verify the entitlement, then reopen the Pro screen if needed.

Manage Apple subscriptions or use Apple’s Report a Problem service for eligible refund requests. Deleting PaceShift does not cancel a subscription.
