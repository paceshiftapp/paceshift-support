Inclusive access

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 18, 2026

PaceShift is designed to make timer setup, active sessions, history, insights, purchases, privacy choices, and support usable with Apple accessibility features.

App accessibility goals

  • VoiceOver labels, values, traits, and a predictable focus order for key controls.
  • Larger Text and accessibility text sizes without hiding essential actions.
  • Light and dark appearance, increased contrast, and information not conveyed by colour alone.
  • Reduced Motion support for non-essential animation.
  • Generous touch targets and clear control names for timer actions.
  • Responsive portrait and supported landscape layouts on iPhone and iPad.
  • Accessible purchase, restore, permission, support, and deletion paths.

Website accessibility

This support site uses semantic headings, keyboard-accessible links, visible focus indicators, a skip link, scalable text, light and dark colour schemes, reduced-motion support, and no required JavaScript. It is designed to remain readable when zoomed or viewed on a small screen.

System interfaces

Some experiences use Apple-provided interfaces, including the App Store purchase sheet, Apple Health, Calendar, notifications, Apple Watch, and—when included—the Family Activity Picker. Their accessibility behaviour is partly controlled by the operating system.

Report an accessibility barrier

Email paceshift.app@gmail.com and include, when possible:

  • the screen, page, or feature;
  • the action you were trying to complete;
  • the assistive technology and settings in use;
  • your device model and operating-system version;
  • the PaceShift version and build; and
  • a concise description of the barrier.

Do not include payment-card details or unnecessary health information.
