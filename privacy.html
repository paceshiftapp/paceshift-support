Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 18, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled by PaceShift, its iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, widget, and Live Activity experiences, and this support website.

1. Developer and Privacy Officer

PaceShift is developed and published by Gavin Naicker, an independent developer based in British Columbia, Canada (the “Developer”). Gavin Naicker is also the designated PaceShift Privacy Officer.

Privacy, access, correction, complaint, and deletion requests involving information the Developer controls can be sent to paceshift.app@gmail.com.

PaceShift does not require a separate PaceShift account and does not operate a social network, advertising profile, or data-broker service.

2. Privacy summary

PaceShift does not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or use cross-app tracking. The app does not include a third-party advertising or analytics SDK. Most app information stays on your devices. Selected continuity information may use your private Apple iCloud/CloudKit database when that feature is enabled and available.

4. Local processing and iCloud continuity

Most PaceShift processing occurs on your device. When iCloud continuity is enabled and available, PaceShift may use Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud database to keep eligible timer configuration and expressly supported Focus or Study information available across your own devices.

PaceShift’s continuity filter is designed to exclude completed HIIT or fitness sessions, heart-rate values, heart-rate zones, workout personal records, Apple Health imports, and health-bearing aggregates from PaceShift’s own CloudKit continuity snapshot. Apple controls the iCloud service, account security, and storage-management interfaces.

5. Apple Health

PaceShift requests Health access only when you deliberately use a Health-related feature. Depending on the feature and permissions you grant, the iPhone app may read eligible heart-rate or workout information, and the Apple Watch app may read live heart rate and save an eligible completed workout to Apple Health.

PaceShift does not use Health information for advertising, marketing, data brokerage, or eligibility decisions. You can review or revoke access using Apple’s Health and privacy controls. Deleting PaceShift does not automatically delete records already saved in Apple Health.

6. Bluetooth heart-rate monitors and Calendar

PaceShift may receive heart-rate measurements from compatible Bluetooth Low Energy monitors that expose the standard Heart Rate service. Measurements are used for the session features you select and are handled on device. PaceShift does not claim support for cycling power, cadence, speed, smart trainers, foot pods, or ANT+ sensors.

Calendar access is optional and is used only when you deliberately choose Calendar context for a supported session. You can revoke access in Apple device settings.

7. Guarded Protection and Screen Time

This section applies only when Guarded Protection appears in your installed version. When available, Guarded Protection is an optional free capability and does not require PaceShift Pro. It uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks and requires your deliberate individual authorization.

Apple’s picker represents your selection using opaque tokens. PaceShift does not use those selections for advertising, profiling, or sale and is not designed to read the content of selected apps, browsing history, or screen contents. You can change the selection, disable Guarded Protection, or revoke authorization using Apple device settings.

8. Purchases

PaceShift Basic is free. PaceShift Pro uses Monthly and Annual auto-renewable subscriptions. Apple processes purchases and supplies verified entitlement information to the app. The Developer does not receive your full payment-card number or Apple Account password. The App Store purchase sheet is authoritative for your localized price, taxes, renewal terms, and introductory-offer eligibility.

9. Sharing and service providers

PaceShift does not sell personal information. Information is handled by service providers only as needed for features you choose:

  • Apple: App Store and StoreKit, iCloud/CloudKit, HealthKit, Calendar, notifications, widgets, Live Activities, Apple Watch connectivity, and—when included—Family Controls/Screen Time. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google: Gmail processes correspondence sent to paceshift.app@gmail.com. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub: GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process network, security, and service logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Any third party that receives personal information from PaceShift is required, through applicable service terms or other binding obligations, to provide the same or equivalent protection described in this policy and to use the information only for the relevant service purpose. Apple services that you use directly also operate under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

PaceShift does not operate a developer-controlled advertising, cross-app tracking, or session-replay server.

10. This website

This website uses static HTML and CSS. It contains no developer-added analytics, advertising, cookies, contact form, remote fonts, or JavaScript. GitHub may process ordinary hosting and security logs. When you choose an email link, your email provider and Gmail process the message under their respective policies.

11. Retention and deletion

  • Local app information remains until you delete it using available PaceShift controls, delete the app and its data, or reset the relevant feature.
  • Private CloudKit continuity information remains until you remove it through available PaceShift or Apple iCloud controls.
  • Apple Health records remain under Apple Health controls until you remove them.
  • Apple retains transaction records according to Apple’s legal, fraud-prevention, and accounting obligations.
  • Local diagnostics are bounded and pruned and remain local unless you choose to export or send them.
  • Support correspondence is reviewed periodically and retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain an appropriate support record, prevent abuse, resolve a dispute, or meet a legal obligation.

Detailed removal instructions are provided on the Data Choices & Deletion page.

12. Your choices and rights

You may decline optional permissions and still use the core timer, although the related feature will not work. You can manage Health, Calendar, Bluetooth, notifications, Live Activities, iCloud, and—when applicable—Screen Time access through PaceShift or Apple device settings.

For personal information the Developer controls, you may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting the Privacy Officer. Reasonable identity verification may be required. Some information may be retained where permitted or required by law.

Privacy concerns should first be sent to the PaceShift Privacy Officer. You may also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia at info@oipc.bc.ca.

13. Security

PaceShift uses platform protections such as Apple sandboxing, permission controls, private CloudKit storage, App Groups for related PaceShift components, and StoreKit verification. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your devices and Apple Account protected and avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for support.

14. International processing

Apple, Google, and GitHub may process information in Canada, the United States, or other countries where they or their service providers operate. Information processed outside your province or country may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction.

15. Children and families

PaceShift is a general-audience productivity and fitness timer and is not designed specifically for children. A parent or guardian should supervise a minor’s use and permission choices where appropriate. Do not use PaceShift to monitor another person without authority or consent.

16. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PaceShift, its service providers, or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be revised. Material changes will be communicated in the app or on this site when appropriate.
